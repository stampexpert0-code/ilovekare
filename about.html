Who We Are

About iLoveKare

A trusted name in concierge-style senior care since 2013. Led by founder Kimberly Haase, we are dedicated to helping aging adults maintain independence, dignity, and joy throughout Cherokee and Cobb County.

Meet Our Founder Kimberly Haase
Our Founder

A Passion Born from Experience

Kimberly Haase founded iLoveKare with a deeply personal mission: to ensure that every senior has access to compassionate, dignified care that feels like family. Drawing from her own experiences navigating the care landscape for loved ones, she recognized a profound need for personalized, concierge-level support that treats each client as a unique individual, not a case number.

What began as a heartfelt commitment to serving aging adults in the community has grown into a trusted concierge care service recognized throughout Cherokee and Cobb County. Under Kimberly's leadership, iLoveKare has built a reputation for excellence — combining professional expertise with genuine warmth to create care experiences that truly matter.

Today, iLoveKare continues to be guided by the same principles that inspired its founding: trust, compassion, and an unwavering dedication to the dignity of every person we serve.

We don't just provide care — we build relationships rooted in trust, respect, and genuine compassion. Every client becomes part of our extended family.
Kimberly Haase
Founder & Chief Executive Officer

Companion Care

Our Philosophy

Care That Feels Like Family

We believe that exceptional senior care goes far beyond assistance — it is about building meaningful, lasting connections. Every interaction is an opportunity to bring comfort, dignity, and joy to the lives of those we serve. Our approach is personal, thoughtful, and always centered on the individual.

At iLoveKare, we understand that aging is not a condition to be managed, but a journey to be honored. We meet each client where they are, respecting their history, their preferences, and their dreams for the future. This is not just care — it is companionship, advocacy, and genuine human connection.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

These principles guide every decision we make and every relationship we build. They are the foundation of the iLoveKare experience.

Compassion First

Every interaction is guided by empathy and genuine care. We see the person first, not the need — treating each client with the same warmth we would offer our own family.

Unwavering Trust

We earn trust through transparency, consistency, and integrity. Families invite us into their homes and their lives — we honor that privilege with absolute reliability.

Excellence in Everything

From the first consultation to daily care visits, we pursue excellence without compromise. Our BBB A+ rating reflects a standard we refuse to lower.

Personalized Approach

No two clients are alike, and neither are their care plans. We design bespoke support that honors individual routines, preferences, and personal history.

Safety & Security

Every team member is thoroughly vetted, bonded, and insured. We create safe environments where seniors can thrive with confidence and families can rest easy.

Dignity Always

We believe aging with dignity is a right, not a privilege. Every service we provide is designed to preserve independence, self-respect, and quality of life.
