Our Journey

The iLoveKare Story

From a single act of compassion to a trusted name in concierge senior care. Discover how Kimberly Haase built iLoveKare on a foundation of empathy, dedication, and an unwavering belief in dignified aging.

The Beginning

A Seed of Compassion

The inspiration behind iLoveKare blossomed while Kimberly assisted her great-grandmother, Ella Colemburg, and their circle of friends. These kind souls recognized Kimberly's compassionate senior care and affectionately bestowed upon her the nickname "little missionary."

Driven by her desire to provide personalized lifestyle care for her grandmother and friends, Kimberly embarked on a journey that would eventually lead to the creation of iLoveKare — a service dedicated to offering home care services to those in need.

I was always inspired to take my education seriously and believed it was the way to achieving success and my life purpose for what God want me to do.
Our Journey

Key Milestones in the iLoveKare Story

From early inspiration to community leadership, each chapter of our story reflects a deepening commitment to compassionate senior care.

1998

The Spark

Kimberly embarked on her journey as a live-in director at a personal care home. Her role ignited a passion for providing compassionate senior care within the aging community.

2000s

Advocacy & Growth

As she served the elderly, Kimberly discovered a calling to advocate on their behalf. She realized that to truly make a difference, she needed to establish her own platform for home care services.

2013

iLoveKare is Born

With over 20 years of experience, Kimberly officially launched iLoveKare — a concierge-style senior care service designed to help aging adults maintain independence, dignity, and joy in the comfort of their own homes.

Today

A Trusted Name

iLoveKare continues to serve families throughout Cherokee and Cobb County with personalized lifestyle care, tech training, safety prevention, and companion services — all delivered with the warmth and professionalism that defines our brand.

Our Growth

Building Something Meaningful

Kimberly's desire to offer personalized lifestyle care for the elderly led her to serve not only her own family and acquaintances but also countless others over the course of more than 20 years. She recognized a unique niche in assisting aging adults to age in place — a mission she continues to champion today.

At iLoveKare, we are committed to providing compassionate senior care that enables our clients to maintain their independence and mobility. Our home care services extend beyond medical care, focusing on creating a personalized lifestyle care plan that fosters a comfortable and healthy environment for each individual. We strive to alleviate caregiver burnout and promote independence for all.

Recognized By Cobb County, GA
Recognition

ALL IN Cobb Innovator Award 2023

Kimberly Haase was honored with the prestigious ALL IN Cobb Innovator Award, presented by Chairwoman Lisa Cupid. This recognition celebrates visionary leaders who are making a tangible difference in the Cobb County community through innovation, compassion, and dedicated service.

The award reflects not just Kimberly's individual commitment, but the collective impact of the entire iLoveKare team in transforming how senior care is delivered throughout the region.
