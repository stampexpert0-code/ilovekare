Our Services

Personalized Support for Every Need

From daily companionship to technology training, travel assistance to home management — we provide thoughtful, concierge-level care designed around your life.

Core Service

Companion Care

Meaningful companionship and daily assistance to help seniors stay engaged, active, and connected to the world around them.

  • Daily check-ins and friendly conversation
  • Mobility and activity support
  • Customized to your schedule and preferences
  • Emotional support and social engagement
Education

Tech Training & Support

Patient, judgment-free technology coaching to help seniors confidently navigate the digital world — from video calls to online shopping.

  • One-on-one personalized classes
  • Small group sessions (up to 10)
  • On-site or in-home instruction
  • Computer, phone, and internet skills
  • Zoom, Skype, and FaceTime guidance
Lifestyle

Travel Concierge

Safe, escorted travel arrangements and day-trip assistance so seniors can explore, attend events, and visit loved ones with confidence.

  • Travel planning and booking assistance
  • Escorted support during events and trips
  • Local travel and day trips
  • Safe, comfortable transportation
Convenience

Errands & Deliveries

Reliable grocery runs, personal shopping, and dry cleaning — with curbside and no-contact options for added safety and convenience.

  • Grocery pick-up and drop-off
  • Curbside and no-contact delivery
  • Personal shopping assistance
  • Dry cleaning and laundry services
Home Care

Home Management

Light home maintenance oversight, organization, and coordination so life at home stays effortless, safe, and comfortable.

  • Waiting for cable or repair services
  • Scheduling and overseeing home maintenance
  • Closet and space organization
  • General home oversight and tidying
Wellness

Meal Preparation

Thoughtful meal planning, grocery shopping, and kitchen care — keeping nutrition simple, enjoyable, and tailored to dietary needs.

  • Grocery shopping for fresh ingredients
  • Meal prep and cooking assistance
  • Kitchen cleaning and disinfecting
  • Dishwashing and kitchen organization
Transition

Hospital & Rehab Transition

Seamless discharge-to-home support — from pickup and safe escort to medication collection and settling in comfortably.

  • Pick-up at hospital or rehabilitation center
  • Safe escort home with transportation
  • Medication pick-up at pharmacy
  • Home settling and comfort coordination
Support

Personal Assistant

Daily administrative and communication support — managing calls, emails, appointments, and video check-ins with professionalism.

  • Daily companionship and wellness check-ins
  • Video conferencing setup and support
  • Phone calls and email management
  • Appointment scheduling and reminders
Remote

Virtual Support

Remote assistance for families and seniors — from technology troubleshooting to virtual companionship and family video updates.

  • Remote technology troubleshooting
  • Virtual companionship calls
  • Family video updates and check-ins
  • Online account and password assistance
